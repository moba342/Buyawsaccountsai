# Commercial activation boundary — separate from SEO

This filename is retained for existing links. It is **not an SEO verification prerequisite**. The earlier Product Verification & Publishing Rules have been removed from the SEO workflow.

`catalog-seo.js` and `seo.js` prepare transactional metadata, crawlability and structured catalog facts from existing records without documents. `offer-publication.js` controls only actual commercial authorization, availability, scope and purchase activation. It no longer returns indexability or metadata-proposal decisions and does not import SEO proposals.

The existing commercial review map remains empty. All 68 purchase actions and payment checks stay protected; categories retain their commercial status. SEO and sitemap preparation succeed nonetheless. No request for verification documents is needed to develop, test or review SEO. Commercial records may be managed in their own workflow when activation is requested; they cannot be fabricated for SEO.

Listed prices are retained as supplied catalog facts and distinguished from credit face value. No stock, verified status, delivery promise, support guarantee or invented service is generated. Existing real payment integration, wallet addresses, QR codes, receipt validation and cart state are unchanged.
