# Technical indexability report

All **14 categories and 68 variants** now have `index, follow` by default, self-referencing HTTPS/www canonicals and inclusion in the **94-URL sitemap**, independently of commercial reviews. An explicit editorial `seoOverrides[id].noindex` still removes a URL. Cart/checkout remain crawlable `noindex, follow`, are absent from sitemap, and unknown routes retain genuine noindex 404s. APIs remain disallowed in robots.

This is technical eligibility, not a guarantee that Google indexes every page or that a product is purchasable. Existing 68 distinct introductions, specification tables and substantive buying guides provide page-specific content. Any future duplicate/thin content can be individually improved or explicitly noindexed without changing purchase eligibility. No commercial document approval is read by the sitemap or robots decision.

The release is source-only in this task. Current production continues serving the previously approved release until a separate deployment action. Historical 12-URL evidence is preserved under `evidence/production/`; current evidence belongs under `evidence/independent-seo/`.
