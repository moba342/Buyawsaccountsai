# Professional storefront redesign

The redesign keeps the Midnight Sapphire palette, supplied logos, fonts, catalog data, routes, contact information, and existing sales-inquiry flow.

- Refined the shared header, footer panels, homepage framing, provider cards, and product collection.
- Added purpose-specific introduction panels to Compare, Guides, and FAQ pages.
- Added a comparison shortlist counter and a clear-selection action.
- Created a shared purchase-summary panel for all provider and individual account pages.
- Reworked supporting information into lighter, readable panels with consistent icons and spacing.
- Reorganized the contact introduction around the information buyers should include in an inquiry.
- Preserved sample-review disclosure and existing account-check guidance; no unsupported certification or provider partnership claims were introduced.

Validation: production build; 1,152 page/viewport checks without reported failures; accessibility scans with zero detected violations; direct routes, refresh/history and legacy URL checks; comparison reset/limits; provider and product inquiry dialogs; guide dialogs and FAQ expansion; 200% text scaling; 4,803 links across 72 pages, limited to internal navigation and Telegram/email/phone contact.
